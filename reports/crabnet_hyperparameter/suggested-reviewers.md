# Suggested Reviewers

**Manuscript:** Materials Science Optimization Benchmark Dataset for High-dimensional,
Multi-objective, Multi-fidelity Optimization of CrabNet Hyperparameters

**Authors:** Sterling G. Baird, Jeet N. Parikh, Taylor D. Sparks

**Target journal:** *Integrating Materials and Manufacturing Innovation* (IMMI)

This list prioritizes researchers with a **confirmed prior IMMI publication record**, since
editors strongly prefer reviewers familiar with the journal's scope (ICME, materials data,
optimization, uncertainty quantification, manufacturing innovation). Candidates were also
screened for topical fit to the manuscript (CrabNet / composition-based deep learning,
Bayesian / adaptive-design optimization, multi-objective and multi-fidelity methods, and
benchmark datasets) and for conflicts of interest. The shortlist was generated with the
assistance of an Edison Scientific high-effort literature query (FutureHouse PaperQA;
task `add4359b-3898-48c3-9366-8c7bf1746765`) and the supporting IMMI citations are recorded
below. Email addresses and current affiliations should be confirmed against each researcher's
institutional page before submission (addresses intentionally not fabricated here).
