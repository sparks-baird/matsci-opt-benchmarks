# Suggested Reviewers

**Manuscript:** Materials Science Optimization Benchmark Dataset for High-dimensional,
Multi-objective, Multi-fidelity Optimization of CrabNet Hyperparameters

**Authors:** Sterling G. Baird, Jeet N. Parikh, Taylor D. Sparks

**Target journal:** *Integrating Materials and Manufacturing Innovation* (IMMI)

This document lists suggested reviewers for the CrabNet hyperparameter benchmark dataset
manuscript. Candidates were selected for expertise in materials informatics, Bayesian /
adaptive-design optimization, and benchmarking for materials discovery, with a preference
toward researchers whose work intersects integrated computational materials engineering
(ICME) and manufacturing innovation, matching IMMI's scope. Suggested reviewers were
screened to exclude co-authors and recent collaborators of the authors (see
[Conflicts of interest](#conflicts-of-interest-to-exclude)). Email addresses should be
confirmed against each researcher's current institutional page before submission.

## Recommended reviewers

1. **Raymundo Arróyave** — Department of Materials Science & Engineering, Texas A&M University, USA.
   - *Expertise:* Multi-fidelity and multi-information-source Bayesian optimization for
     materials design; ICME; uncertainty quantification.
   - *Relevance:* Directly works on the multi-fidelity / multi-information-source optimization
     framing central to this dataset, and is highly active in the ICME / manufacturing-innovation
     community that IMMI serves.

2. **Anubhav Jain** — Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, USA.
   - *Expertise:* Materials informatics, high-throughput computation, and benchmarking of
     materials property-prediction methods (lead author of the Matbench benchmark suite).
   - *Relevance:* The dataset is built on the Matbench experimental band gap task; an authority
     on materials ML benchmark design and reproducibility.

3. **A. Gilad Kusne** — National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), USA.
   - *Expertise:* Autonomous materials discovery, active learning, and benchmarking of
     optimization strategies for materials.
   - *Relevance:* Has explicitly benchmarked active-learning / optimization strategies for
     materials discovery and brings a measurement-science and manufacturing perspective aligned
     with IMMI.

4. **Tonio Buonassisi** — Department of Mechanical Engineering, Massachusetts Institute of
   Technology, USA.
   - *Expertise:* Bayesian optimization and autonomous experimentation across multiple
     experimental materials-science domains; benchmarking of optimization performance.
   - *Relevance:* Co-led cross-domain benchmarking of Bayesian optimization in materials science,
     closely related to the benchmarking objectives of this work.

5. **Gian-Marco Rignanese** — Institute of Condensed Matter and Nanosciences, UCLouvain, Belgium.
   - *Expertise:* Robust model benchmarking, data-driven materials science, and bias/imbalance in
     materials datasets (MODNet).
   - *Relevance:* Provides expertise on rigorous benchmarking methodology and data-quality issues
     relevant to constructing a fair surrogate/benchmark dataset.

6. **Keith A. Brown** — Department of Mechanical Engineering, Boston University, USA.
   - *Expertise:* Self-driving labs, autonomous experimentation, and Bayesian optimization
     benchmarking.
   - *Relevance:* Strong background in the experiment-planning and optimization-benchmarking
     context, bridging optimization methods and physical/manufacturing workflows.

7. **Kedar Hippalgaonkar** — Nanyang Technological University / A*STAR, Singapore.
   - *Expertise:* Accelerated materials discovery, machine learning, and high-throughput /
     autonomous experimentation.
   - *Relevance:* Active in benchmarking Bayesian optimization for materials and in
     manufacturing-relevant accelerated discovery, fitting IMMI's scope.

8. **Bryce Meredig** — Citrine Informatics, USA.
   - *Expertise:* Industrial materials informatics, ML model evaluation, and best practices for
     benchmarking ML in materials science.
   - *Relevance:* Provides an industry / manufacturing-innovation perspective on dataset utility
     and benchmarking rigor, well matched to IMMI's audience.
